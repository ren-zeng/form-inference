render :: forall a nt t l ann. (Num l, Ord l) => (Block a nt t -> ann) -> Tree (Block a nt t) -> Tree (RigidBlock l ann nt t) #

calculates the dimensions of blocks in a tree based on the tree structure - the total width of a block is the sum of widths of its gaps and the sum of the widths of its terminals (all of size 1) - the height of a block's hole is the largest combined height of its child block - the height of a block's cap is (for leaf blocks) its distance to max depth (for node blocks) always 1
